# MemeZo Platform Strategy

## Phase 1: Launch Reliably

Use replaceable providers for banking, payments, KYC, bills, cards, custody,
market feeds and messaging. Keep secrets server-side, verify webhooks, reconcile
money movement and fail closed whenever an audited adapter is unavailable.

## Phase 2: Own The Intelligence

Persist raw observations, normalized features, model versions, explanations and
5/10/30-minute outcomes. Build proprietary Runner, Risk, Whale, Smart Money,
Momentum, Social Sentiment and Holder Growth scores.

## Phase 3: Build The Data Network

Operate internal market aggregation, multi-chain discovery, holder analytics,
wallet intelligence, social signal and trade-outcome services. Provider payloads
must terminate at adapters so downstream services use MemeZo-owned contracts.

## Phase 4: Offer Commercial APIs

Expose authenticated, metered APIs for token discovery, risk scoring, runner
scoring, wallet intelligence, multi-chain analytics and AI explanations.

## Phase 5: Become An Ecosystem

- Consumer: mobile app, web app and WhatsApp Assistant.
- Business: merchant automation, business accounts and team wallets.
- Developer: MemeZo APIs, intelligence feeds and automation services.

Every internal service should be versioned, observable and reusable outside the
mobile app without exposing provider secrets or payment authority.
